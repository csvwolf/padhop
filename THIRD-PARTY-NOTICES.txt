The reference preset preserves existing haptics and button assignments. It disables smoothing, inertia, click-stability pause and drag slowdown, and sets wheel distance to 6000 with fine scrolling. System wheel-line normalization and horizontal scrolling are not ported; Windows scroll settings still affect the result.

Haptic adaptation also references src/app/ControllerManager.cpp and src/steam/SteamController.cpp at the same commit. Reference defaults: command 1 TICK / command 2 CLICK at 0 dB, 50 ms minimum movement interval, 6500 raw units travel, 45-unit deadzone, 4000-unit maximum step, area gate 900. Press/release confirmation and touch/release/idle grace periods are converted from upstream 250 Hz frame counts to elapsed time (8/16/48/180/120 ms) for Bluetooth. These timing conversions and this app's pressure-based mouse click mapping are adaptations, not a claim of identical device behavior. User edits can override waveforms, cadence, grace periods, and pressure gate. The app keeps each actuator separate and a single pending feedback slot instead of a backlog.
